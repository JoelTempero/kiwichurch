Who We Are

More than a Sunday service

Kiwi Church is a network of intentionally small communities meeting in homes across Christchurch. We share meals, wrestle with honest questions, and walk through life together.

We're not trying to be the biggest church in town. We're trying to be a community where everyone is known, everyone belongs, and everyone has a seat at the table.
